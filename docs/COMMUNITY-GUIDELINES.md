## 1. Введение

> Текст для публикации:

```
Welcome to BestMe!

BestMe is a wellness community where people share their goals, progress,
and inspiration. To keep our community safe and supportive, everyone must
follow these Community Guidelines.

These rules apply to all content on BestMe, including posts, photos,
comments, messages, profiles, and community pages.

By using BestMe, you agree to follow these guidelines. Violations may
result in content removal, account warnings, or permanent suspension.
```

## 2. Что такое BestMe

```
BestMe is a social platform focused on wellness, personal growth, and
healthy living. Our community includes:

- Personal users sharing wellness goals and progress
- Certified wellness professionals and coaches

Our mission is to create a positive, supportive environment where everyone
can improve their wellbeing.
```

## 3. Запрещённый контент

> **Обязательно для Apple §1.2, Google UGC Policy, DSA Art. 14**

```
The following content is strictly prohibited on BestMe:

ILLEGAL CONTENT
• Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) — zero tolerance, immediately
  reported to authorities (NCMEC)
• Terrorist content or recruitment
• Content promoting illegal activities
• Intellectual property violations (copyright, trademark)

HARMFUL CONTENT
• Hate speech — content attacking individuals or groups based on race,
  ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or
  national origin
• Threats, bullying, or harassment — including doxxing (sharing
  someone's private information)
• Violence or graphic content — including threats of violence
• Non-consensual intimate images

HEALTH & SAFETY
• Content promoting eating disorders (pro-ana, pro-mia)
• Dangerous health practices or unproven medical treatments presented
  as cures
• Content encouraging self-harm or suicide
• Sale of controlled substances, drugs, or unapproved supplements

DECEPTION
• Spam or misleading content
• Fraud, scams, or phishing
• Impersonation of other people or organizations
• Fake reviews or ratings of wellness businesses
• Misleading health claims (e.g., "this cures cancer")

SEXUAL CONTENT
• Nudity or sexually explicit content
• Sexual solicitation

PRIVACY
• Sharing others' personal information without consent
• Recording or sharing private conversations without consent
```

## 4. Правила для wellness-контента

> **Специфика BestMe как wellness-платформы:**

```
WELLNESS CONTENT GUIDELINES

BestMe is a wellness platform. We have additional rules for health-related
content:

ALLOWED:
✅ Sharing personal wellness journeys and progress
✅ General wellness tips (exercise, nutrition, mindfulness)
✅ Certified professionals sharing educational content
✅ Linking to peer-reviewed research
✅ Sharing personal experiences with health conditions
✅ Motivational and inspirational content

NOT ALLOWED:
❌ Diagnosing medical conditions
❌ Prescribing treatments or medications
❌ Claiming products cure diseases
❌ Promoting extreme diets or dangerous exercise practices
❌ Before/after photos that promote unhealthy body standards
❌ Selling prescription medications
❌ Presenting personal opinion as medical fact

DISCLAIMER REQUIREMENT:
If you share health-related advice, you must include a disclaimer:
"This is not medical advice. Consult a healthcare professional."

Certified wellness professionals may share professional advice within
their area of certification but must clearly identify their credentials.
```

## 7. Как работает модерация

> **Обязательно по DSA Art. 14 — описать процесс модерации**

```
HOW WE MODERATE CONTENT

BestMe uses a combination of technology and human review to enforce
these guidelines:

1. AUTOMATED SYSTEMS
   We use AI-powered tools to automatically detect and remove:
   - Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
   - Spam and fake accounts
   - Nudity and explicit content

   Automated systems may make mistakes. If your content is incorrectly
   removed, you can appeal the decision (see section 10).

2. USER REPORTS
   Any user can report content that violates these guidelines.
   Reports are reviewed by our moderation team.

3. HUMAN MODERATORS
   Our team reviews flagged content and makes final decisions.
   All appeals are reviewed by a human moderator.

TRANSPARENCY:
- We will notify you if your content is removed, with the reason
- You have the right to appeal any moderation decision
- We publish annual transparency reports on our moderation activity
```

## 8. Последствия нарушений

```
ENFORCEMENT

Violations of these guidelines result in the following actions:

LEVEL 1 — CONTENT REMOVAL
• First minor violation
• Content is removed
• You receive a notification with the reason

LEVEL 2 — WARNING
• Repeated minor violations or first serious violation
• Content is removed + formal warning
• Warning is recorded on your account

LEVEL 3 — TEMPORARY SUSPENSION
• Multiple warnings or serious violation
• Account suspended for 7-30 days
• Cannot post, comment, or message

LEVEL 4 — PERMANENT BAN
• Severe violations (CSAM, terrorism, repeated harassment)
• Account permanently suspended
• Cannot create new account

IMMEDIATE REMOVAL (no warning):
• CSAM — immediate removal + law enforcement report
• Terrorist content — immediate removal
• Direct threats of violence — immediate removal
• Doxxing — immediate removal

Note: The severity of the response depends on the nature and frequency
of violations. We consider context when making moderation decisions.
```

## 9. Как подать жалобу (Report)

> **Обязательно по Apple §1.2, Google UGC, DSA Art. 16**

```
HOW TO REPORT CONTENT

If you see content that violates our guidelines:

1. Tap the ••• (more) button on the post, comment, or profile
2. Select "Report"
3. Choose the reason:
   - Spam or misleading
   - Hate speech or discrimination
   - Violence or threats
   - Child exploitation (CSAM)
   - Bullying or harassment
   - Nudity or sexual content
   - Fraud or scam
   - Dangerous health misinformation
   - Other
4. Add details (optional)
5. Submit

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER YOU REPORT:
• You receive a confirmation that your report was received
• Our team reviews the report within 24 hours
• You will be notified of the outcome

Your identity is kept confidential — the reported user will NOT know
who reported them.
```

## 10. Как обжаловать (Appeal)

> **Обязательно по DSA Art. 20**

```
HOW TO APPEAL

If your content was removed or your account was suspended, you can
appeal the decision:

1. Go to Notifications → find the removal notice
2. Tap "Appeal this decision"
3. Explain why you believe the decision was incorrect
4. Submit your appeal

WHAT HAPPENS:
• Your appeal is reviewed by a human moderator (not the same person
  or system that made the original decision)
• You will receive a response within 48 hours
• If the appeal is approved, your content is restored

You can appeal within 6 months of the original decision.

If you disagree with the appeal outcome, you may contact us at
appeals@bestme.app or use an out-of-court dispute resolution body
(for EU users, as per DSA Art. 21).
```
